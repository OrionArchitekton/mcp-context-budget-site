# MCP context budget product demo

A developer can watch scan, select and budget enforcement before installing the
tool. The demo reuses the released package's bundled synthetic fixture and real
recorded CLI output. It identifies the demonstrated version, synthetic inputs,
estimated token counts, edited replay and synthetic narration. Fixture counts
do not imply provider tokenization accuracy, model quality or measured savings.

- A short 16:9 recording shows the catalog, task selection, a passing selected
  lock and rejection of the full catalog under the same cap.
- The microsite exposes a native player, poster, English captions and transcript
  before the setup instructions, with and without JavaScript.
- Playback starts on visitor interaction; the full video does not preload.
- Media works through the Works subpath and supports seeking and fullscreen.
- Existing installation, source links and product information remain usable.
- The source script, captured output, demonstrated release and media hash are
  preserved so a later release can decide between reuse and refresh.

Test seams: built production HTML and packaged media, then desktop/mobile
playback, captions and seeking with JavaScript enabled and disabled. Public
availability is established only by playback at the final Works URL after the
approved site merge/deployment. Creative acceptance remains with Dan.
